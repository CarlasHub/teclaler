# Portuguese Year 1 manual research (October 2026)

This is a review of **public publisher descriptions, catalogues and previews**, not a claim that five licensed books were read cover to cover. Full digital manuals require purchase or school access. No textbook text, artwork, exercises or ordered word lists were copied into TeclaLer.

The [Direção-Geral da Educação's Aprendizagens Essenciais](https://www.dge.mec.pt/aprendizagens-essenciais-ensino-basico) are the primary curriculum reference. The app's practice sequence is an original draft: five image-supported words, 25 regular short words, 14 words introducing accents and nasal vowels, then 12 words with digraphs or consonant groups. The sequence, syllabification, pronunciation and the accessibility of the tasks need a Portuguese Year 1 educator's review before a supervised child pilot.

The current Portuguese recordings were synthesized locally with the free, Apache-2.0 licensed [Qwen3-TTS](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS). Separate synthetic literacy-teacher reference voices keep `pt-PT` and `pt-BR` pronunciation distinct; no real person's voice or child recording is used. The model files stay in the development workflow and the app ships normalized WAV clips. The clips remain marked `needs_review` and should be listened to by native speakers before testing with children.
