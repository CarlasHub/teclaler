# Word illustrations

The `word-*.svg` files copied from OpenMoji 17.0.0 are by the [OpenMoji contributors](https://openmoji.org/) and are licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Their code points and word mapping are in `src/curriculum/wordIllustrations.ts`. They are used as supplied, without edits. The `word-mesa.svg`, `word-toma.svg` and `word-trapo.svg` illustrations are original TeclaLer drawings.

Run `npm run images:import` to fetch the pinned OpenMoji SVGs. The app serves the committed local copies and needs no network access for pictures.
