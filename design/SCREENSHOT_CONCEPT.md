## Behaviour shown

The concept shows the desired learning flow:

1. The child sees `CASA`.
2. The child sees the syllables `CA` and `SA`.
3. The child types into boxes: `C A S _`.
4. The current key is highlighted on the keyboard.
5. The app displays/hears: `Ouves: “cê”`.
6. The replay button says `Ouvir outra vez`.
7. When the word is finished, the app reads `casa`.

## Implementation goal

Preserve the hierarchy and behaviour, not every pixel.
